- Official PL explanation: https://www.premierleague.com/en/european-qualification-explained (retrieved 2026-10-18).
- Four UCL league-phase places are the base allocation; a fifth place depends on the season's association-coefficient European Performance Spot (EPS).
- FA Cup and EFL Cup winners qualify for Europa League and Conference League respectively, subject to the official redistribution rules. If a fifth UCL berth is earned, the Europa League league-position slot moves down the table as the official rules describe. Cup winners/UEFA titleholders also affect distribution.
- Therefore no fixed 5th/6th-place UEL/Conference band is presented as secured in this in-season table. Positions 1–4 are labeled as base UCL positions, with the fifth-place EPS caveat. The bottom three are marked for relegation, per the official PL explainer: https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4365156/new-to-the-premier-league-heres-all-you-need-to-know.
- Dashboard source list includes these official rules; qualification remains subject to changes in UEFA access-list conditions.
